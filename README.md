\# 🚨 JeevaRaksha AI



\### AI-Powered Emergency Communication \& Assistance System



JeevaRaksha AI is an AI-powered emergency communication and assistance system designed to help people communicate emergency situations clearly when they are confused, injured, unable to explain the situation properly, or facing accessibility difficulties.



The system accepts \*\*text, voice, and image inputs\*\* and combines them to understand the emergency situation, estimate its severity, identify supporting evidence, and generate a clear emergency summary and shareable alert.



> ⚠️ JeevaRaksha AI is an assistance system. It does not replace emergency services, medical professionals, or professional first responders.



\---



\## 🎯 Problem



During emergencies, people may:



\- Be injured or confused

\- Have difficulty explaining what happened

\- Be unable to type a detailed message

\- Face language or accessibility barriers

\- Need to communicate important emergency information quickly



Traditional emergency communication can require the person to describe the situation clearly under stressful conditions.



\---



\## 💡 Our Solution



JeevaRaksha AI provides a multimodal emergency assistant that accepts:



\- 📝 Text input

\- 🎤 Voice input

\- 📷 Image input



The AI Emergency Engine analyzes the available information and produces:



\- Emergency situation classification

\- Risk severity level

\- Risk factors

\- Text evidence

\- Visual evidence

\- Multimodal evidence agreement

\- Emergency summary

\- Shareable emergency alert
